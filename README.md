## Description
This project aims to analyse customer sales patterns and performance trends across multiple branches, demographics, and product lines for Cadre Autos, an automobile sales company. Using Microsoft Excel, an interactive dashboard was developed to track total sales, customer demographics, brand and model performance, and time-based sales trends. Through this analysis, the dashboard provides clear visibility into sales performance, enabling Cadre Autos to optimise sales strategy, improve branch performance, and maximise revenue while strengthening overall business decision-making.

### Business Introduction
Cadre Autos is a multi-branch automobile sales company operating across several states, offering a wide range of vehicle brands and models. The company serves diverse customer segments and competes in a highly dynamic automotive market.
As sales volumes increased, Cadre faced challenges in tracking performance across branches, understanding customer demographics, and identifying top-performing brands and models. This made it necessary to adopt a centralised, analytical reporting approach.

### Business Problem
Cadre Autos is currently facing the following challenges:
- Limited visibility into sales performance across branches and locations
- Difficulty identifying high-performing car brands and models
- Lack of insights into customer age and gender trends
- Poor tracking of seasonal and monthly sales patterns
- Manual reporting processes are slowing strategic decisions.

### Rationale for the Project
Understanding sales performance is critical for growth and competitiveness. This project was developed to:
- Provide a single source of truth for sales reporting
- Identify profitable branches and customer segments
- Track product performance by brand and model
- Reveal peak sales periods and demand patterns
- Support smarter, faster business decisions.

### Aim of the Project
This project aims to develop an interactive Excel dashboard that enables Cadre Autos to:
- Monitor total and average sales performance
- Analyse sales by branch, state, and time period
- Understand customer demographics and buying behaviour
- Identify top-selling car brands and models
- Improve strategic planning and sales optimisation.

### Project Scope and Workflow
Data Preprocessing
- Cleaned and validated raw sales data
- Standardised dates, age ranges, and categories
- Removed duplicates and corrected inconsistencies

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analysed sales by branch and state
- Examined customer age and gender distribution
- Identified top-performing brands and models
- Investigated monthly and weekly sales trends

Data Transformation
- Grouped customers into age ranges
- Created time-based fields (month, weekday)
- Aggregated sales metrics for dashboard use

Data Analysis
- Compared branch-level performance
- Evaluated brand and model contribution to total sales
- Analysed demographic-driven purchasing behaviour
- Identified peak sales days and months

### Insights Generated
Key insights from the dashboard include:
- Lagos branch records the highest sales volume
- Customers aged 24–33 years are the strongest buyers
- Toyota and Ford are the top-performing brands
- Sales peak in November, with a sharp drop in December
- Thursdays and weekends show higher sales activity
- Gender-based sales distribution is relatively balanced

### Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:
- Focus marketing on high-performing age groups
- Increase inventory for top-selling brands and models
- Strengthen underperforming branches
- Align promotions with peak sales months
- Optimise staffing based on daily sales patterns.
